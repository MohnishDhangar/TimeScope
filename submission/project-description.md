# TimeScope

TimeScope is a public web tool for exploring genuine NASA SPHEREx observations of the same sky region across acquisition times. Enter right ascension and declination, choose a field width and observation count, or open a bundled real-data sequence. Play the dated timeline, blink observations, compare synchronized panels and inspect wavelength, coverage and original FITS provenance. Download an HTML evidence card with embedded previews for offline review.

The application makes temporal image inspection accessible to students, educators and citizen scientists. Approximate wavelength recommendations expose an important confounding factor: images from different dates may also sample different infrared wavelengths. Apparent changes are not automatically motion or variability. No discovery or quantitative detection is claimed.

Live discovery depends on IRSA. When discovery fails transiently, only a validated inventory for exactly the requested region may be reused, with its original timestamp and limitations. Bundled sequences remain accessible independently of fresh archive searches. See the target-validation report for individual challenge-target outcomes.
